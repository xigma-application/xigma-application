Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Who we are

Xigma is a private, self-hosted design tool. Your data is kept only on the servers that run Xigma, controlled by its owner. Contact: xigma.sender@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, an optional name and, if you choose one, a profile picture.
  • Password: only as a one-way hash (argon2id); the password itself is never stored.
  • Google sign-in: when you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address, whether Google has verified it, your name and your profile picture. We ask only for the basic openid, email and profile permissions and never get access to your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • Sessions: a sign-in cookie, the device's browser name and IP address, so you can see and end your sessions.
  • Your work: the files, designs and comments you create in Xigma.

How we use it

Only to run Xigma for you: to sign you in, keep your account secure, send account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password) and show your work. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone, except where the law requires it.

Cookies

Xigma uses cookies that are needed to sign you in and keep you signed in, and one that remembers your cookie choice. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Your account data and work are kept until you delete your account. After deletion, your account and its sessions are removed and you lose access to the files you created with it.

Your rights

You can see and change your account data, end your sessions and delete your account at any time. You can also remove Xigma's access to your Google account in your Google Account settings, under Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services. For anything else, write to xigma.sender@gmail.com.

Google user data

Xigma's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
