Xigma

Xigma is a design tool for teams who build products together: design files, prototypes and comments in the browser.

Xigma is a private, self-hosted project. It runs on its owner's own computers and is not offered as a public service. You can sign in with an email and password or with your Google account.

Read how Xigma handles your data in the Privacy Policy and the rules of use in the Terms of Service.
